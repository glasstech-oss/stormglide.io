# Case Study Templates - 5 Ready to Complete

Each case study needs to tell a complete story: Problem → Solution → Results → Learnings.

---

## Case Study Template Structure

### Part 1: Client Profile (100-150 words)
- Company name and industry
- Company size/stage (employees, revenue, years in business)
- Key business challenges before engagement
- Why they approached StormGlide

### Part 2: The Problem (200-300 words)
- Specific pain points (quantified where possible)
- What wasn't working and why
- Cost of the problem (time lost, money lost, missed opportunities)
- What they tried before

### Part 3: The Solution (300-400 words)
- What was built (features, architecture, integrations)
- How we approached the problem
- Technologies used
- Key decision points
- Why this solution was right

### Part 4: Implementation (150-200 words)
- Timeline to launch
- Team composition
- Key milestones
- How the client was involved in the process

### Part 5: Results (200-300 words)
- Quantified outcomes (time saved, money saved, efficiency gains, user adoption)
- How the business changed
- ROI calculation
- Unexpected benefits

### Part 6: Key Learnings (150 words)
- What we learned about their industry
- What worked well
- Advice for similar companies

### Deliverables (100 words)
- What was delivered
- Technology stack
- Support arrangements
- Scalability notes

## Case Study Interview Guide

### Before the Interview
1. **Prep the client**: Send them the questions 1 week before
2. **Gather background**: Check your project notes, timelines, costs
3. **Research metrics**: What would be valuable to show?

### During the Interview
1. **Record (with permission)**: Makes it easier to quote accurately
2. **Ask follow-ups**: "Can you give me a number?" "What changed?" "How do you measure that?"
3. **Get emotional**: "How did this make you feel?" "What was the hardest part?"
4. **Get specific**: "Can you give me an example?" "What specifically changed?"

### After the Interview
1. **Transcribe**: Pull key quotes and metrics
2. **Structure**: Match to the case study template
3. **Fill gaps**: Ask follow-up questions via email if needed
4. **Draft**: Write the story
5. **Review with client**: Let them verify accuracy and tone
6. **Publish**: Add to case study page with photo/logo if available
