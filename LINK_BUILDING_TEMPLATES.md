# Link Building for Ghana SEO - Email Templates & Outreach List

## Overview

Link building is 30% of SEO ranking. For Ghana-focused keywords, we need:
- Ghana tech media backlinks (TechPlus Ghana, Graphic Tech, etc.)
- Ghana business directories (local citations)
- African tech platforms (TechCrunch Africa, Disrupt Africa)
- Global SaaS directories (G2, Capterra)
- Industry-specific links (HR tech, logistics, ecommerce)

Timeline: 12 weeks of outreach, 2-3 new links per week = 24-36 links total

```

[TIER 1 VARIANT: One thing that makes StormGlide different is that we build for offline-first environments. In Ghana and across Africa, internet connectivity isn't guaranteed, so our software works without internet and syncs when you're online. This is a real advantage for companies in logistics, manufacturing, and field operations.]

I think there's a strong story here about:
- African companies building software FOR African companies
- The unique requirements of African markets (offline capability, local payment integrations, low-bandwidth optimization)

Would you be open to a conversation or interview? I can share details about our products, talk about our approach, or provide a case study from one of our customers.

Looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
StormGlide
[Phone]
[Email]
[Website]
```

```
I'm [Your Name] from StormGlide, and I'd love to contribute a guest post to [Publication Name]. Here's what I'm thinking:

**Title**: "Building SaaS for African Markets: What's Different — And Why It Matters"

**Angle**: Most SaaS advice is written for US/Europe companies with stable internet, credit cards, and English-speaking users. But African companies have different requirements:
- Offline-first design (not just offline fallbacks)
- Local payment integrations (Paystack, MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash)
- Low-bandwidth optimization (users on 2G networks)
- Multi-currency and tax complexity

This article would explore these challenges and share lessons from building Nexus HRM, CargoScan, and other products that 100+ companies use.

**Audience fit**: Your readers interested in SaaS, African tech, and operational software would find this valuable.

**Length**: 1500-2000 words, includes 2-3 case examples

Would this interest you? I can send a full outline and first draft next week.

Best,
[Your Name]
StormGlide
```

## Follow-Up Strategy

**No response after 5 days**: Send follow-up email
```
Hi [Name],

Just checking if you got my email from [date]. Would love to discuss [opportunity].

No pressure if it's not a fit right now — but let me know if there's anything I can clarify.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**No response after 10 days**: Try alternative contact (if available)

**No response after 14 days**: Move on and note as "no response"

## Measuring Success

- **Target**: 24-36 new links in 12 weeks = 2-3 per week
- **By tier**:
  - Tier 1: 4-6 links
  - Tier 2: 6-8 links
  - Tier 3: 6-8 links
  - Tier 4: 6-8 links

- **Quality over quantity**: 1 link from TechCrunch Africa > 10 links from small directories

- **Track**:
  - Links acquired
  - Traffic from each link
  - Ranking improvement for target keywords
  - Referral conversions (contacts from link clicks)

## Pro Tips

1. **Personalize every email**: Use their name, reference their recent articles, show you know their publication
2. **Make it easy**: Provide all info needed (quotes, images, case studies, facts)
3. **Multiple angles**: If one pitch doesn't work, try a different angle
4. **Timing**: Tuesday-Thursday are best for emails (not Monday chaos, not Friday ignored)
5. **Follow publication guidelines**: Some only take guest posts, some only feature news
6. **Offer value first**: "Here's something your readers would love" beats "cover us"
7. **Build relationships**: Think long-term, not one-off link
8. **Document everything**: Make replicable outreach process for future team member
