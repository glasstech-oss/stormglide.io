The situation today

Right now, HR at most companies runs on spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages and paper forms — leave requests chase people down by phone, onboarding checklists live in someone's memory, and if a laptop goes missing, nobody can say for certain who has it.

The platform Stormglide built replaces all of that with one secure system that every employee, manager and director logs into — where every request follows the same automatic approval chain, every document is generated and branded on demand, and every action is logged. What follows is a walkthrough of exactly what it does, module by module.

Everything included

Nine modules. One system.

Every capability below is live in the platform today — organized the way an HR team actually uses it, day to day.

People & Records

Every employee's full record lives in one verified place instead of scattered files.

  • Central employee database — role, rank, department & reporting line
  • Live organization chart that updates as people move
  • Secure document vault per employee, with digital signing
  • One-click official personnel dossier as a branded PDF

Time, Attendance & Leave

No paper leave forms, no "who approved this?" — every request is auditable automatically.

  • Attendance tracking, including biometric kiosk support
  • Multi-stage leave approval: hand-off → manager → HR
  • Real-time leave balances & medical certificate uploads
  • Branded Leave Authorization Certificate, generated instantly
  • Company holiday calendar built in

Onboarding, Offboarding & Assets

New hires get a guided checklist, not a scavenger hunt — and every laptop is tracked from day one.

  • Onboarding checklists auto-split across HR, IT, Marketing & the manager
  • IT equipment assignment against a live asset inventory
  • ID card & building access card issuance, tracked end to end
  • Structured offboarding so no asset gets left behind

Performance & Growth

Goals, reviews and promotions happen on a schedule the system enforces.

  • Company, department & individual KPI / goal setting
  • Structured appraisal cycles with executive sign-off
  • Continuous check-ins & 360° feedback
  • Calibration tools so ratings stay fair across teams
  • Promotion & probation tracking with clear dates
  • Predictive analytics to flag performance risk early

Policy, Discipline & Compliance

Every policy has proof someone read it, and every case has a paper trail.

  • Central Policy Library with required, trackable acknowledgment
  • Disciplinary case management, issue through resolution
  • Full audit log — who did what, and when — across the system

Payroll & Finance

Pay, expenses and staff loans run through one controlled process, not a spreadsheet.

  • Payroll runs & digital payslips
  • Staff expense claims and approvals
  • Compensation & benefits management
  • Staff loans and advances, tracked against payroll

Employee Support & Communication

Questions land in the right inbox automatically, instead of getting lost in chat.

  • IT / HR / Finance helpdesk with SLA timers & auto-routing
  • Built-in knowledge base for common questions
  • Company-wide announcements & a personal notifications inbox

Recruitment & Training

Hiring and staff development live in the same system as everything else.

  • Applicant & recruitment pipeline tracking
  • Training programs logged against each employee's record

Reporting, Documents & Governance

Leadership gets a real-time picture — and every document looks like it came from your brand.

  • Live dashboards & predictive analytics, by department and company-wide
  • Every export — certificates, dossiers, appraisals, ID batches — a branded PDF
  • Full company branding control: your logo, your colors, everywhere
  • Bank-grade encrypted records with role-based permissions on every screen
The walkthrough

The employee journey, start to finish

Five stages, one continuous system — this is the actual path every hire follows inside the platform.

1
Hire

HR creates the employee record. Their login stays disabled until preboarding is complete — nothing is accessible before it should be.

2
Onboard

IT provisions accounts and equipment, Marketing issues the ID card and publishes the digital call card, and the line manager runs induction — each on their own checklist, assigned automatically the moment the record is created.

3
Work

Attendance, leave, support tickets and daily targets all flow through the same system — visible to exactly the people who need to see them, no more, no less.

4
Grow

Goals, appraisals, 360° feedback and promotions run on a set cycle the system enforces — not whenever someone remembers to start the process.

5
Move On

A structured offboarding checklist recovers every asset and closes every account before the final day — nothing quietly falls through the cracks.

See it in motion

Watch a leave request move itself

A real workflow already built into the platform — one click at each stage, and nothing advances until the right person has actually acted on it.

Step 1 Employee submits Dates, reason and days remaining are pulled in automatically from their balance.
Step 2 Manager reviews The line manager sees it in their queue and approves or declines, with a comment.
Step 3 HR gives sign-off Final review confirms coverage and department impact before it's locked in.
Step 4 Certificate issued A branded Leave Authorization Certificate is generated and ready to download — automatically.
A request that used to mean four phone calls and a paper trail nobody could find later now takes one click at each stage — and every step is timestamped and logged.
