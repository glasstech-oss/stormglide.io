## SEO Strategy

### Keyword Targeting Strategy
**Two-Tier Approach:**
1. **Global Pages** (No "-ghana" suffix)
   - Compete for broad, high-volume keywords
   - Attract international clients
   - Build topical authority
   - Example: "software development company" (global audience)

2. **Location Pages** (With "-ghana" suffix)
   - Capture local search intent
   - Rank for geo-specific keywords
   - Build local authority
   - Example: "software development company ghana" (local audience)

**No Cannibalization:**
- Homepage targets both (geo-aware)
- Global pages target primary keywords
- Ghana pages target local keywords
- Internal linking structure prevents dilution
